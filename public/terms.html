2. What MaskMate is

MaskMate is a free, open-source, fully client-side PII sanitisation tool. It detects and redacts patterns resembling personal data (emails, phone numbers, Aadhaar, PAN, credit card numbers, IP addresses) using pattern-matching rules that run entirely in your browser. It is not a legal compliance service.

7. Sample / demo data
